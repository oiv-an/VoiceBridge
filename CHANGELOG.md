## 0.10.4 - 2026-09-02

Initial public release.

- Push-to-talk Russian, English, and Vietnamese voice translation.
- Optional local Russian and Vietnamese speech recognition with downloadable models.
- On-device ML Kit translation, back-translation, pronunciation hints, and matching-language speech playback.
- VND detection with selectable RUB, USD, EUR, and UAH estimates.
- Optional floating, on-device screen translation into Russian.
- Public-release privacy disclosures, third-party notices, Google attribution, and signed APK packaging.
